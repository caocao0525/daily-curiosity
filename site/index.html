This theoretical work determines when the Holevo rate is achievable for visible compression of finite memoryless quantum sources with known classical labels. The criterion involves the purity of signals on the quantum part of each sector in the Koashi–Imoto decomposition. For qubits, this means signals are pure or commute pairwise.
