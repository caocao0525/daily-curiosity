Explainable ML reveals chromosome 1-specific chromatin dynamics under serum starvation

설명 가능한 머신러닝으로 혈청 결핍 시 염색체 1의 특이적 염색질 역학을 밝혀냈다.

Using CRISPR-Sirius time-lapse imaging and machine learning with Shapley value explanations, this study uncovers that chromosome 1 loci uniquely move toward the nuclear envelope during serum starvation, maintaining constrained dynamics and exhibiting long jumps at the periphery. This chromosome-specific response contrasts with overall nuclear morphology changes and chromatin dynamics, highlighting differential chromatin reorganization mechanisms.

Why it matters: It provides a novel explainable ML approach to dissect chromosome-specific chromatin dynamics, advancing understanding of spatial genome regulation under stress.
