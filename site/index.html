These findings reveal spatial-temporal patterns of white matter disruption associated with BD phases and brain activity changes.

Vacuum sandwiched Rényi mutual information as CFT partition functions on complex manifolds

In any d-dimensional conformal field theory (CFT), the vacuum sandwiched Rényi mutual information (SRMI) of integer order n between two disjoint balls corresponds to the CFT partition function on a manifold with topology #^{n-1}(S^1 × S^{d-1}), with moduli fixed by the ball configuration. At n=2, it equals the thermal partition function on S^{d-1}, bounding the von Neumann mutual information. For n>2, SRMI diverges at finite separation due to hot-spot limits. Explicit CFT calculations match density matrix computations.

Why it matters: This work connects entanglement measures in CFTs to partition functions on complex topologies, providing rigorous tools for quantum information and theoretical physics.
