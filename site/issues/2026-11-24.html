CAMEO is an adversarial learning framework that fuses multimodal biomedical data from small, partially paired datasets by integrating pretrained unimodal encoders with cross-modal latent alignment. Evaluated on computational pathology tasks like niche classification and cell type prediction, CAMEO outperforms contrastive methods such as CLIP in low paired-data regimes, demonstrating superior data efficiency. The approach enables learning shared representations with minimal paired samples, facilitating multimodal integration where paired data is scarce.
