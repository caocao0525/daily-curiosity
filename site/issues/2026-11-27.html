REGAL framework explicitly programs tissue-specific enhancer regulatory grammar across species

REGAL은 조직 특이적 증강자 조절 문법을 명시적으로 설계하여 기능을 예측하고 검증함.

The REGAL method uses reinforcement learning to explicitly design enhancer regulatory grammar—combinatorial transcription factor motif arrangements—under sequence-to-function oracles. REGAL-generated enhancers across species showed tissue-specific expression patterns validated experimentally in mouse embryos. The study reveals that tissue specificity depends on coordinated activation and repression, motif composition, and spatial organization, with distinct regulatory strategies across tissues.

Why it matters: By explicitly modeling enhancer grammar, REGAL advances understanding and design of tissue-specific regulatory elements, a key challenge in genomics and gene regulation.

Local-global spherical sliced Wasserstein graph kernel achieves 84% accuracy in schizophrenia identification

뇌 기능 연결성 그래프 커널로 조현병 진단 정확도 84% 달성.

The study proposes the LG-SSWG kernel, embedding functional connectivity profiles onto a hyperspherical manifold and using geodesic optimal transport to construct a positive-definite graph kernel. Evaluated on the COBRE dataset, it achieved 84% accuracy in schizophrenia classification, outperforming baselines. The method identifies clinically interpretable neuroanatomical biomarkers such as the left thalamus and right precuneus.

Why it matters: This approach advances neuroimaging analysis by integrating geometric and functional information for improved schizophrenia diagnosis and interpretability.
