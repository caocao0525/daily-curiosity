What DNA Foundation Models Learn Beyond Sequence Composition

DNA 기초 모델은 단순 서열 구성 이상의 기능적 패턴을 포착한다.

This study evaluates frozen embeddings from five DNA foundation models against k-mer frequency baselines across 57 genomic classification datasets and gene-expression tasks. It finds that simple k-mer features remain competitive, but the NTv3 model consistently outperforms them. Decomposing embeddings reveals that linear predictability from k-mers does not guarantee downstream utility, and sequence-order sensitivity is task-relevant. Thus, foundation models capture functional patterns beyond simple sequence composition.

Why it matters: This work rigorously quantifies the incremental predictive value of genomic foundation models beyond classical k-mer features, informing model selection and interpretation in computational genomics.
