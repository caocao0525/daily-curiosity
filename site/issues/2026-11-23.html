Transcriptome-aware tokenization reduces cost in single-cell foundation models

scTAT은 단일 세포 모델에서 유전자 토큰화를 효율적으로 개선한다.

Single-cell foundation models usually treat each gene as a token, which is computationally expensive and inefficient due to many low-expression genes. scTAT introduces transcriptome-aware tokenization by grouping co-expressed genes into overlapping gene sets that compete with single genes for token positions per cell, normalizing expression scores by set size. This dynamic per-cell tokenization reduces token count and computational cost while preserving informative gene expression patterns for model pretraining.

Why it matters: This method innovatively reduces tokenization complexity in single-cell foundation models, improving scalability and efficiency in computational genomics.

This study models cortical spreading depression (CSD), a wave of neuronal depolarization linked to migraine aura and brain injury, on smooth Riemannian 2-manifolds representing cortical geometry. Replacing the Euclidean Laplacian with the Laplace-Beltrami operator, the authors prove well-posedness, derive curvature-corrected eikonal relations showing wavefront speed modulation by geodesic curvature, and establish criteria for sulcal trapping of waves. This geometric approach captures effects of cortical folding on CSD propagation.

Why it matters: It rigorously integrates cortical geometry into reaction-diffusion modeling of neural phenomena, advancing computational neuroscience and mathematical modeling of brain dynamics.

Preschool children use choice-relative moral reasoning considering alternatives

유아들도 선택 가능한 대안을 고려해 도덕 판단을 한다는 것을 보여주었다.

Using a child-friendly trolley problem, 3- to 5-year-olds judged a protagonist's action of moving a barrier to protect cookies. Children rated the action as morally good when no better alternative existed, even if harm occurred, but as morally bad when a non-harmful alternative was available but not chosen. This indicates preschoolers evaluate actions relative to available alternatives, not just outcomes, demonstrating early development of choice-relative moral reasoning.

Why it matters: This study provides rigorous experimental evidence that young children incorporate counterfactual alternatives in moral judgments, informing evolutionary psychology and cognitive development.

Preprint-level caution: behavioral study with young children; interpretations should consider developmental variability.
