LSD1 Links Metabolism to Stemness in Human Neural Stem Cells

Lysine-specific demethylase 1 (LSD1) regulates metabolic programs essential for stemness in human fetal neural stem cells. LSD1 inhibition shifts metabolism towards glycolysis and alters lineage commitment, suppressing neuronal differentiation and promoting astrocytic fate. Machine learning identified metabolic genes regulated by LSD1, and chromatin immunoprecipitation confirmed LSD1 occupancy at these loci, linking epigenetic regulation to metabolic control of stem cell fate.

Why it matters: This study reveals a mechanistic link between epigenetic regulation and metabolism in neural stem cells, highlighting LSD1 as a key factor integrating chromatin state and metabolic pathways relevant to stemness and differentiation.

scDORI: Deep Multi-omic Regulatory Inference Decodes Glioblastoma Plasticity

scDORI는 단일세포 다중오믹스 데이터를 활용해 교모세포종의 세포 가소성을 조절하는 유전자 네트워크를 해독합니다.

scDORI is a deep-learning framework that infers enhancer-driven gene regulatory networks at single-cell resolution from multiome data. Applied to over one million glioblastoma cells, it reveals a hierarchical and predictable regulatory landscape governing cellular plasticity, with asymmetric activator and repressor interplay. Perturbation screens validate key regulators, showing cross-lineage trajectories absent in normal neurodevelopment, advancing understanding of glioblastoma progression.

Why it matters: This work provides a novel computational method to dissect gene regulatory programs driving cancer cell plasticity at single-cell multi-omic resolution, enabling mechanistic insights into glioblastoma heterogeneity and progression.
