BEND 2.0 is a benchmark suite testing DNA language models on eight tasks from five experimental datasets, assessing their ability to predict effects of DNA variants and guide sequence design. It finds that linear readouts on frozen model representations can match or exceed native outputs, and that functional post-training improves capabilities.
