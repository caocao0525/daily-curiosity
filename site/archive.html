Tree-gram Hierarchical n-gram Models as Controlled Language Approximators

트리그램은 계층적 n-그램 모델로, 언어 모델의 용량과 문맥 범위를 명확히 조절하며 효율적인 생성 모델을 구현한다.

This work introduces tree-grams, autoregressive hierarchical n-gram models that build conditional probabilities via compositions of order-2 and order-3 tensors. Tree-grams control maximal context window and model capacity, outperforming existing n-gram baselines and competing with deep transformers. They enable explicit control of horizon and capacity, showing emergent sparsity and allowing pruning of up to 95% of topologies without performance loss.

Why it matters: Tree-grams provide interpretable, scalable approximations of language models with controlled capacity, relevant for understanding and designing efficient generative models potentially applicable to biological sequence modeling.

VRLLM: Fine-tuning LLMs for Large-Scale Tabular Classification

사전학습된 LLM을 대규모 표 형식 데이터 분류에 성공적으로 적용하여 산업적 예측에 활용 가능함을 보였다.

Visa Research fine-tuned a pretrained Llama 3.2 1B LLM on billions of labeled tabular data rows, achieving performance surpassing many tree-based and neural tabular classifiers. The study details data preparation, training recipes, ablation studies, and interpretability experiments showing feature-specific predictive associations. Early feedforward layers contribute to linearly decodable task information, demonstrating that compact LLMs can be adapted effectively for industrial tabular prediction tasks.

Why it matters: This shows that pretrained LLMs can be adapted to large-scale tabular data classification, a domain relevant for computational genomics datasets and biological feature matrices, expanding the applicability of foundation models.

OAK-Mind: Zero-Training Multi-Agent LLM Pipeline for Mental Health Change Inference

지식 기반 다중 에이전트 LLM을 활용해 훈련 없이 수동 감지 데이터로 정신 건강 변화를 추론한다.

OAK-Mind is a zero-training, multi-agent knowledge-grounded LLM pipeline that infers mental health changes from passive sensing data without task-specific fine-tuning. It organizes behavioral data into dimensions like sleep and activity, uses specialized agents grounded in mental health literature, and synthesizes assessments with questionnaire-based symptom ratings. It matches or outperforms supervised baselines and improves upon direct prompting across multiple LLM backbones.

Why it matters: This approach demonstrates how knowledge-grounded multi-agent LLMs can perform training-free inference on complex behavioral data, suggesting potential for interpretable modeling of biological and psychological signals.

Preprint status; results need further validation.

Symmetry Breaking Dynamics from Long-Range Entanglement in Crosscap States

장거리 얽힘에 저장된 대칭 깨짐이 단위리 동역학에서 국소적으로 나타날 수 있음을 보였다.

The study reveals that unitary dynamics preserving global symmetry can lead to local symmetry breaking when initial symmetry-breaking coherence is encoded in long-range entanglement of charge-deformed crosscap states. Entanglement entropy decreases while entanglement asymmetry increases after a quench, showing that symmetry-preserving dynamics can redistribute symmetry-breaking coherence to make it locally accessible.

Why it matters: This provides new insights into how entanglement structure governs local symmetry properties in quantum systems, relevant for understanding chromatin and epigenomic regulation via long-range interactions.

Theoretical physics study; implications for biological systems are indirect.

Spectral Classification of Random Skew-Kernel Matrices via Free Probability

무작위 스큐-커널 행렬의 스펙트럼 특성을 자유 확률론으로 분류하였다.

This work characterizes the empirical singular spectra of deterministic skew Hilbert–Schmidt kernels evaluated on isotropic random vectors, linking tightness and collapse to quadratic energy conditions. It establishes equivalences between spectral convergence, resolvent behavior, and logarithmic determinants, identifying limiting laws as half-Cauchy distributions. The results apply to polygonal Brownian Lévy-area spectra and provide a framework for analyzing kernel matrices in high dimensions.

Why it matters: Understanding spectral properties of kernel matrices is crucial for analyzing high-dimensional biological data and chromatin interaction matrices, informing computational genomics and epigenomics methods.
