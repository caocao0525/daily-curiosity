In any d-dimensional conformal field theory (CFT), the vacuum sandwiched Rényi mutual information (SRMI) of integer order n between two disjoint balls corresponds to the CFT partition function on a manifold with topology #^{n-1}(S^1 × S^{d-1}), with moduli fixed by the ball configuration. Explicit CFT calculations match density matrix computations.

Why it matters: This work connects entanglement measures in CFTs to partition functions on complex topologies, providing rigorous tools for quantum information and theoretical physics.
