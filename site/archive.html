Waist inequality and a Hurewicz-type theorem for mean dimension

This paper proves a Hurewicz-type inequality relating mean dimension and conditional metric mean dimension in dynamical systems. It shows that for any equivariant continuous map, the mean dimension of the domain is bounded by the sum of the mean dimension of the target and the conditional metric mean dimension of the map. The authors also establish a new waist inequality applicable to finite simplicial complexes of arbitrary topology, which may have broader geometric implications beyond dynamical systems.

Why it matters: It advances rigorous mathematical foundations of mean dimension theory and provides a novel geometric inequality, valuable for researchers in computational topology and dynamical systems.
